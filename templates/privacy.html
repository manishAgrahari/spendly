Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

1. Information we collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and the expense records you create (amount, category, date, and description).

2. How we use your information

  • To provide and maintain your account and expense data.
  • To generate summaries, category breakdowns, and spending insights within the app.
  • To communicate important updates about the service.
  • To improve and secure the platform.

3. How we store and protect data

Your data is stored securely and access is restricted to what is necessary to operate the service. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your expense records with third parties except where required by law or necessary to operate the service (for example, hosting infrastructure).

5. Your choices

You can review, update, or delete your expense data at any time from within your account. You may also request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

6. Cookies

Spendly may use essential cookies to keep you signed in and to remember your preferences. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@spendly.app.
